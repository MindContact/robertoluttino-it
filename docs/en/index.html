20 Objects that respond

My speciality is physical interfaces. I take an ordinary object, such as a desk, a set of mechanisms or a board, and make it interactive. Sensors and electronics disappear inside the object, which stays as it was but now notices who is using it and responds. A screen is something you look at; an object is something you touch, open, move. Perception widens, instead of shrinking to a glowing rectangle.

I follow the whole technical chain: sensors, electronics, firmware and software. For the set-up I work with professionals in the field.

30 Roots

Ivrea

I studied computer science at the ITIS C. Olivetti technical school in Ivrea, Adriano Olivetti's town: an idea of enterprise where technology, culture, design and community belong together. It is still a reference.

Theatre

At the MultiDams in Turin I studied with Antonio Pizzo, Vincenzo Lombardo and Edoardo Fadini: interactive drama as a system of rules, multimedia theatre, video theatre, new theatre.

Officine Sintetiche

In 2008 and 2009 I took part in the Officine Sintetiche workshops and found interactive systems: the one thing that held all my passions together.

Reading

Myron Krueger, Brenda Laurel, Steve Mann and his “humanistic intelligence”, Studio Azzurro, Lev Manovich and “augmented space”.

40 Passions

Electronics

Commodore 64, then MS-DOS, Amiga 1200, Windows, and finally Linux and embedded systems. I never quite left the C64.

Music

Music and theatre are the heart of my studies. Today the music comes out of an emulated SID chip, with hexline.

Games

As teenagers, Demis and I had a group for inventing video games. Today I make platformers and chiptune tools that run in the browser, with an 8-bit soul, on mindcontact.io.
